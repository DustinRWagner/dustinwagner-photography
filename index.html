THE PROCESS

Shot manual. Edited by hand.

Every frame is exposed manually on a Canon EOS with the EF-S 18–55mm kit lens, then finished by hand in post: crop, tone, contrast, and nothing that changes what was in front of the camera. The one composite in the archive is labeled as a composite.

No AI-generated imagery. No stock. No borrowed frames.

About the photographer

I'm Dustin Wagner, an economics student in Folsom, California who has kept a camera within arm's reach since 2024. Photography started as a class and became a discipline: a standing commitment to go out, look harder at ordinary places, and come back with something worth keeping.

The 70 frames on this page were selected from a much larger working archive. They cover eight series, wildlife, night work, macro, motion, architecture, portraiture, found scenes, and a working archive, built up over two years of steady shooting.
