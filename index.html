THE PROCESS

Shot with intent. Edited by hand.

Most frames are exposed manually on a Canon EOS with the EF-S 18–55mm kit lens, and every frame is finished by hand in post: crop, tone, contrast, and nothing that changes what was in front of the camera.

No AI-generated imagery. No stock. No borrowed frames.

About the photographer

I'm Dustin Wagner, an economics student in Folsom, California, and I've kept a camera close since 2024. Photography started as a class and turned into a habit of going out, looking harder at ordinary places, and trying to come back with something worth keeping.

The 73 frames on this page were selected from a much larger working archive. They span ten series, macro, architecture, motion, night work, material studies, black-and-white object studies, found scenes, portraiture, the high country, and work made abroad, built up over more than two years of steady shooting at home in Folsom and on the road.
